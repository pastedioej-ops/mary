my dearest, mary;

i do not know when this letter will reach you, but i hope it finds you well. still, i found myself thinking of you tonight and thought perhaps i ought to write. i’m so scared. the noise and the flashes never stop, and it feels like the ground is falling apart. but every time i close my eyes, i just see your face. you’re the only reason i'm holding on. i don't know what happens next, but i promise that i am fighting with everything to win this war. i will get back to you. i will come home.

char, HWAHAHAHAH.

i am writing to remind you that i still do like you, mary. i find it cheesy to call it “love” because i am afraid that i may be moving too fast. but i want you to know that i do think it is love. i like you both platonically and romantically. it is hard to trust me, given what you have seen of my personality, and i understand why you feel that way. and you might not think of this, but i do wonder about your “trust issues” often because i don't think of them as a joke. i apologize if my avoidant habits have made you hesitant to trust me. but i am willing to do the work and change my behavior.

detachment was simply a tool i used to have in order to keep myself “safe.” however, i didn't realize how selfish this could be. i never really thought of it as wrong, not even a tiny bit. but thank you for making me realize that appreciation was something i took for granted. value was something i couldn't seem to recognize in people. i saw vulnerability as a weakness. i thought being known was hideous. i thought of my soul as dirty and empty, as though it had been left to rot. what i find funny is that i might have just been exaggerating things. you were right, and that made me laugh. it didn't make me realize it immediately, but you slowly made me realize that.

you're kinder than the stars i have wished upon. you remind me of how things are supposed to be.

to tell you the truth, i think of you as brave. don't get me wrong; i am not being sarcastic. i just think that you have this bravery in you when it comes to showing how embarrassing and cowardly you can be.

you are shy, you easily get lost, you're afraid of being alone, you overthink, you have this fear of missing out bisag di ka ganahan naay kapareha, at times you find it hard to dismiss what bothers you, you try so hard to be nonchalant, you're often unmotivated to move, it's like the cat gets your tongue every time you've had a thought, you don't care much about how you dress, you often space out, you can't hold eye contact, you use humor to brush off compliments, very praning ug baba, and you're very particular about hygiene. i don't remember much, but there were times i saw you stand so nervously sa tubangan, and you would refuse to face the front from way before pa jud. WHAHAHAHA. talawan kayka do, but i've also never seen anyone so raw before. your honesty and bareness caught me off guard; they made me strip away my own defenses. the things i've mentioned are mostly balik-balik because i can't name any more. there are more things to love about you that i have yet to discover.

however, i am the opposite of you. i don't cherish things. i don't overthink. i don't care much about things. i don't know how to appreciate. my view of things was plainly as objects, regardless of whether they breathed. emotions were toxins that i thought would make me drown. i saw the things i wanted as possessions. i used people as “test subjects” to learn more about the way the mind works, and i simply lacked empathy.

i am completely different from you. so why is it that i am slowly changing?

was it your bare personality that made me want to change? or was it simply the way loving you made me realize that i could learn to love deeply, from the tissues of my bones until it reaches the hypothalamus hidden inside this skull? my mind can't think of anything more imaginative, but your fantasies are what i try to see so i can understand you.

i always shut down every part of myself that could make me feel, mary. but ever since i started liking you, i've let them be. they drift up through my mind and reach the muscles of my mouth, making me giggle like a fool. i did not build walls, but simply hid everything deep within. and i wonder, when i finally find the courage to pull out the knife that stabbed this heart of mine, will you think me dirty? will you take a step back when i reek of the rotten stench from the body i buried so long ago?

my mind is often crowded with different voices, but the only constant is the mark your presence has left on me. despite the sharp tingling that spreads through me as the bones in my skull feel as if they would clash together. the cords tied around my throat; you have untangled. a fragility you took out that i did not know of; i haven't yet washed clean.

the sweetness i once despised doesn't taste so bitter anymore, and every piece of it, i will offer to you. i also want to thank you for pulling me back before the worms could settle in my head, chewing through flesh that still tastes of bitterness.

but i have nothing left to give you except these raw thoughts of mine.

i feel things too deeply, and i know how easily you get overwhelmed. so i wonder, as i always do: will this letter catch you off guard? will it scare you away?

but this is something i could do so easily.

i try not to overthink it. i am simply glad to feel gentler. so tell me, is it too much? either way, i really do talk too much. but maybe it's just because i feel safe when talking to you. i might write every day, but only if you are comfortable about it. if you think this is intrusive, let me know. i must know so i can maintain my distance and respect your space.

my thoughts would've told you more, but i must limit my words because it might become too much. i wish you well, mary.

good night,
